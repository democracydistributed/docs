# How to help

DD is volunteer-built. There's something to do whether you have 5 minutes or 5 hours, and most of it needs no coding.

## Pick a way in

| Time | Task | Skills |
|---|---|---|
| 5 minutes | Fix a typo, broken link, or unclear sentence in these docs | None |
| An evening | Trace one payment back to the council decision that authorized it | Curiosity, patience with city websites |
| An evening | Fill a "role not recorded" gap: find who holds a position | Research |
| A few weeks | **Add 2025 Toronto data**: extend the finances to the latest year | Spreadsheets, attention to detail |
| A few weeks | **Add a second city**: bring DD to a new place | Local knowledge, research |
| Ongoing | Improve guides, write explainers, design diagrams | Writing, design |

The two bolded tasks are open because people asked for them.

## How it works

1. **Say hello.** Introduce yourself (see *Get in touch* below) and tell us what you're interested in.
2. **Pick a task** from the list above, or suggest your own.
3. **Do the work** with support. Nobody is expected to know the data model on day one.
4. **It gets reviewed.** Every contribution is checked before it goes live, and you get credit for it.

## The rules that matter most

- **Cite everything.** Every number or claim links to where it came from.
- **Don't guess.** If a link in the chain can't be found, leave it as a gap. A visible gap is more useful than a wrong answer.
- **Facts, not accusations.** DD records what the documents show. It doesn't judge the people involved.

For how to edit these docs, see [CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/democracydistributed/docs/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). Everyone follows the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/democracydistributed/docs/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
